Support around Community Wellness Hub SLC

These services help you access yoga, meditation, and outdoor spaces with ease. Hours may change seasonally, so check provider websites for the latest details.

Service Categories

  1. Transportation

    • UTA TRAX & Bus — Weekdays 5:30 a.m.–11:30 p.m.; Saturday 6:00 a.m.–11:30 p.m.; Sunday limited service
    • GreenBike SLC — Daily 6:00 a.m.–11:00 p.m. (seasonal)
    • Rideshare Drop‑off Zones — Available 24/7 near downtown studios and trailheads

  2. Maintenance & Gear Support

    • Local Bike Shops — Mon–Fri 10:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.; Sat 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.; Sun limited or closed
    • Outdoor Gear Repair — Tue–Fri 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.; Sat 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.

  3. Cleaning & Facility Support

    • Studio Cleaning & Mat Sanitation — Daily before 6:00 a.m. and after 9:00 p.m.; weekly deep clean Sundays
    • Trailhead Restrooms — Spring–Fall 6:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m.; Winter limited access

  4. Community Support & Wellness

    • Community Centers & Rec Programs — Weekdays 7:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.; Sat 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.
    • Local Counseling & Mindfulness Groups — By appointment; many offer evening sessions

  5. Nearby Dining & Refueling

    • Healthy Cafés — Weekdays 7:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; Weekends 8:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.
    • Park City & Jackson Hole Day‑Trip Dining — Many options open daily for breakfast, lunch, and dinner
